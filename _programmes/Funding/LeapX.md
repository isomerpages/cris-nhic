<h4><strong>Partnering to move late-stage innovations towards clinical &amp; commercial inflection point</strong></h4>
<p>The grant is open on a rolling basis.</p>
<p>Focus areas (not limited to): Medical devices, Digital Health, Therapeutics,
Diagnostics</p>
<p>Note: Process innovation projects and productivity improvement tools are
not eligible to apply. The scheme aims to support the development of medium
to high-risk technology projects towards clinical and market deployment.</p>
