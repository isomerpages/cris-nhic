<h4><strong>Partnering to move late-stage innovations towards clinical &amp; commercial inflection point</strong></h4>
<p>The grant is open on a rolling basis.</p>
<p>Focus areas (not limited to): Medical devices, Digital Health, Therapeutics,
Diagnostics</p>
<p>Note: Process innovation projects and productivity improvement tools are
not eligible to apply. The scheme aims to support the development of medium
to high-risk technology projects towards clinical and market deployment.</p>
<h4><strong>Objectives</strong></h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Support the development of late-stage, clinically significant healthcare
innovations that are medium- to high-risk and commercially viable, enabling
them to reach a value inflection point for commercialisation and adoption</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Align stakeholder interests and leverage public and private sector partnerships
to de-risk technologies and accelerate translation</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><strong>Eligibility</strong></h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Principal Investigator must hold primary appointment in a public healthcare
institution and be salaried by the institution in Singapore</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Minimum 20% fresh funding commitment from co-funding partner(s) towards
the total project quantum</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Note: Partner(s) may include local public healthcare cluster/institute,
local public sector entity/national programme office (RIE entity), Industry,
International partner</p>
<h4><strong>Funding quantum and duration</strong></h4>
<ul>
<li>
<p>NHIC’s funding quantum is capped at S$750,000 (inclusive of max. 30% indirect
costs), and will be awarded to the public healthcare host institution on
a reimbursement basis for expenses incurred under the approved proposed
of work.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>
<p>The duration of NHIC funding support is up to 2 years.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<h4><strong>Funding criteria</strong></h4>
<p>Funding is considered if the application satisfies the following criteria:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Well defined, quantifiable and clinically significant unmet healthcare
need</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Medium to high-risk innovative solution, i.e., emerging or deep-tech innovations
with moderate to high development costs. For example, Class B-D or equivalent;
therapeutics, clinical diagnostics, clinical decision-making tools among
others</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The technology has achieved sufficient maturity beyond early technical
feasibility, supported by relevant proof-of-concept, preclinical, pilot,
or early clinical data appropriate to the modality. The proposal should
demonstrate readiness for late stage productisation/ clinical validation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Detailed plan outlining the project deliverables and short to long-term
endpoints including but not limited to: competitive landscape, productisation,
regulatory approval roadmap, clinical validation plan, commercial strategy,
and deployment.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The proposed project scope may include late stage productisation activities
and clinical validation activities.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The applicant team should possess the technical, clinical, operational,
and commercial capabilities required to execute the proposed workplan and
achieve key translational milestones.</p>
</li>
</ul>

<h4><strong>Application process and evaluation</strong></h4>
<p>LeapX applicants must pre-engage with your respective NHIC cluster managers
before proceeding to submit an official application.</p>
<p>Two-stage process</p>
<p>Stage 1: Review</p>
<p>Document required: PowerPoint template-based application</p>
<p>The application may be submitted by the PI directly or through the relevant
cluster-level office/PHI office/co-funding partner via email to <a href="mailto:grant@nhic.cris.sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">grant@nhic.cris.sg</a>.
Please ensure that the key stakeholders are on copy (cluster/institute
level offices and co-funding entity)</p>
<p>Email subject to reflect “NHIC LeapX Application: Add <em>Name of clinical PI (PHI)</em> –
Add <em>Co-funding partner”</em>
</p>
<p>Assessment to check that the minimum criteria is met with clear evidence,
and an independent clinical review to validate the healthcare value.</p>
<p>Stage 2: Evaluation</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Shortlisted applicants will be invited to submit an NHIC supplementary
form to be signed by the PI, collaborators, and endorsed by the public
healthcare institution and co-funding partner.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>NHIC will leverage co-funding partner’s existing project evaluation panel
and mechanism where appropriate, with an NHIC management representative
added as part of the panel to conduct a joint evaluation to finalise funding
decision. If the co-funding entity does not have an existing framework
for a joint evaluation panel, NHIC will convene an evaluation panel.</p>
</li>
</ul>
